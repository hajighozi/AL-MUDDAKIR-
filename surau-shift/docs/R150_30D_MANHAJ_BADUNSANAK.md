## Operational proposal
Raso: listen to needs and feelings. Pareso: verify facts and distinguish observation from inference. Alua: use fair, transparent process with competent responsible adults. Patuik: choose proportionate, respectful response, protect rights. These glosses are proposed teaching interpretations, not final customary definitions. All four applied together; do not infer truth from sentiment or consensus alone.
Kato nan ampek: respectful speech adjusted to interlocutor, with equal dignity; never silence questions. Sato sakaki: participation and voice. Bajanjang naik batanggo turun: staged discussion when appropriate; urgent safety concerns may bypass ordinary group channels to independent adults/services. No compelled mediation or forgiveness. Badunsanak does not replace actual family, legal safeguards or external friendships.
## Example
Two students disagree over a shared book. Raso: hear each account. Pareso: inspect borrowing record without accusation. Alua: teacher facilitates fair turn-taking. Patuik: agree on access and repair if needed. If bullying or abuse suspected, adult safeguarding first, not peer adjudication.
## Proposed learning cycle
20–35 minutes: welcome and opt-in roles, shared reading or observation, discuss using four questions, safe cooperative task, optional feedback. No private disclosures, surveillance, spiritual ratings, financial obligations or pressure to remain. Children have access to trusted adults, family and independent reporting. Assess observable listening, evidence checking, respectful disagreement, equitable participation and task completion; not loyalty or inner state. Test with local adat practitioners, educators and child protection review before pilot; not tested.
