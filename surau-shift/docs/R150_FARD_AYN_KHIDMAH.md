# R-150 addendum — Fard ‘ayn, tazkiyah and khidmah
Status: philosophical direction from user; pedagogical synthesis C/draft, not a fatwa or expert validation. Extends R150_LIFELONG_TAZKIYAH.md.
## Direction
IQRA’ and FAHM orient the learner toward recognition of Allah and responsibility as a servant. Tazkiyah unites heart, intellect, words and deeds throughout life. The heritage of aqidah/kalam, fiqh and tasawuf/akhlaq supplies guidance. Khidmah aims at sincerity, itqan, accountability and service for Allah's pleasure rather than self-serving interests.
## Source audit (limited)
Al-Ghazali, Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din, Rub' al-'Ibadat, Kitab al-'Ilm, chapter 2, section Bayan al-'ilm alladhi huwa fard 'ayn: https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/إحياء_علوم_الدين/كتاب_العلم/الباب_الثاني ; corroborating commentary by al-Zabidi, Ithaf al-Sada al-Muttaqin, vol 1, same section: https://www.islamweb.org/ar/library/content/411/101/بيان-العلم-الذي-هو-فرض-عين . Digital excerpts consulted. Al-Ghazali relates personal obligations to knowledge of required beliefs, actions and abstentions according to circumstances, including remedies for blameworthy states of heart and addressing doubts when needed. This does not make every advanced controversy, entire fiqh corpus, or specialist tasawuf curriculum obligatory on every person. Printed edition/page and complete textual collation pending. No claim of consensus on every classification.
Qur'an 53:32: https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=53&verse=32 warns against claiming purity for oneself. Candidate passages for later tafsir audit: 98:5 (sincerity), 2:30 (khalifah), 28:77 (beneficence and responsibility), 18:110 (righteous deeds). No automatic inference from these to exclusive qualifications for leadership.
## Pedagogical consequences
Core fard 'ayn pathway: necessary aqidah; fiqh of one's current duties and relations; ethics and inner cultivation as needed. Advanced kalam and specialist disciplines are differentiated by need and competence. Study actual texts, circumstances, and boundaries with qualified teachers. Khidmah is both expression and practice of tazkiyah, not a privilege reserved for supposedly purified individuals. Learners may serve safely within capacity while continuing to improve. No spiritual certification, exclusion, shame or grading sincerity. Human dignity does not depend on perceived purity; healthy personal needs, rest and lawful livelihood remain compatible with service. Integrity applies to conflicts of interest, transparent decisions and accountable care. This applies to all learners, not only men.
