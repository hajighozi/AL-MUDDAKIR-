## Proposed competency map (not EQUIP's official inventory)
Public literacy HS01–08: seek trusted support; express needs; recognize ordinary stress without diagnosis; respect privacy; healthy routines; respectful listening; inclusion and anti-stigma; know emergency help pathways. Youth educational only, tell trusted adult; no crisis responder responsibility.
Adult supervised helping HS09–20: consent, explain role, empathy, active listening, open questions, summarize, practical support, collaborative decisions, respectful closure, referral, cultural respect, boundaries. Qualified adult trainers/reviewers assess safe behavior in fictional routine examples, not psychological state or faith; no real patient data.
System HS21–28: accessible education, provider directory verification, cost/eligibility navigation, safeguarding, clinical review, privacy, rights/licensing, non-sensitive aggregate evaluation.
## Psychology discipline candidates
General, cognitive, developmental, educational, social, personality, biological/neuropsychology, health, clinical, counseling, community, cultural, organizational psychology and research methods. Literature classification not clinical accreditation. Separate evidence levels and expert scope. Distinguish mental health conditions, ordinary distress, social problems and neurodevelopmental differences. Public cards: plain-language meaning, misconception, respectful support, professional route; no symptom quiz, diagnosis, treatment protocol or med advice. Clinical pathways exclusively professional. Spiritual support optional, no faith-based causal attribution.
## Delivery
Free low-bandwidth/offline, print and regional language education, no sign-in for reading. Referral routes with verified local eligibility/cost and adult support; urgent danger -> trusted adult/medical emergency services. No guarantee free therapy or cure. No counseling chatbot, patient database, automated triage, sensitive data collection, spiritual or wellness score. Train adult helpers with qualified supervision, not youth as counselors. P3LP != psychotherapy; EQUIP adult manual not youth curriculum. No copyrighted text upload; review license before adaptation. Gate: qualified psychologist/physician and safeguarding review, verified referrals, source and rights audit, age suitability. No clinical deployment, testing or external endorsement. Preserve all earlier IDs and hierarchy.
