# R150.30F — Tark al-Ma‘āṣī through Ikhwan
2026-11-27. Explicit user direction: meninggalkan maksiat adalah misi pertama di surau, dengan sibuk bersama Ikhwan dalam kebaikan ataupun yang mubah. Interpreted as the first mission of Surau Shift, not an entry requirement or ranking of people. Operational elaboration below is proposed, not validated.
## Method
Murabbi provides guidance, care and accountable adult oversight. Ikhwan provide reciprocal companionship and opportunities for constructive, enjoyable, permissible activity. Voluntary shared study, arts, ordinary recreation, rest, meals, safe gardening, sports appropriate to ability, community service and conversations. Mubah activities are legitimate in themselves and may support good intentions; do not claim every permissible act automatically earns worship reward or must become a formal religious task. Avoid compulsory busyness; sleep, solitude, family, outside friendships and autonomy remain protected.
## Candidate scriptural basis for later tahqiq
QS 25:27–29, 43:67, 5:2, 103:1–3; Sahih Muslim 2628 (companionship), Sahih al-Bukhari 1968 (rights of self, family and Lord in Salman–Abu Darda narrative), Bukhari 1 (intentions). These are preliminary locators, not newly verified editions, tafsir or legal rulings. Connect Ghazali Ihya Adab al-Ulfah, chapter 1 and 2, and R150.30E.
## Design and safeguards
Offer meaningful alternatives rather than relying on prohibition alone. Distinguish religious wrongdoing, ordinary habits, emotional distress and clinical conditions. Do not label illness or disability as sin. No claim peer company cures addiction or guarantees avoidance of wrongdoing; professional help remains available. No confession, monitoring private worship, moral scoring, forced activity, shunning, isolation or loyalty requirement. Do not make children responsible for other people's behavior. Provide independent adult protection and choice to decline. Assess accessibility, voluntary participation, observable collaboration and safety, not private sin or faith. Not a treatment protocol.
