## Public source registry
UNICEF LACRO, The 12 Transferable Skills, Jan 2022, official overview inspected: https://www.unicef.org/lac/en/reports/12-transferable-skills . Four dimensions: learning to know (creativity, critical thinking, problem solving); do (cooperation, negotiation, decisions); be (self-management, resilience, communication); live together (diversity, empathy, participation).
CASEL What Is SEL, official page inspected: https://casel.org/what-is-sel/ . Five competencies: self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills, responsible decision-making; SAFE instructional principles: sequenced, active, focused, explicit. School/family/community contexts. Not proof of local efficacy.
UNICEF Indonesia/BKKBN Membantu Remaja Berkembang, March 2026, official Indonesian overview inspected: https://www.unicef.org/indonesia/id/perlindungan-anak/laporan/modul-membantu-remaja-berkembang . Facilitator guide ages 15–19; full module not inspected, no attribution of efficacy.

## Pilot learning units (proposals)
LU-LS01 fact vs opinion: compare fictional claims and source labels.
LU-LS02 communication: ask a trusted adult for help with routine school difficulty; youth never crisis responders.
LU-LS03 cooperative decision: compare options for a fictional school reading corner.
LU-LS04 budgeting: allocate hypothetical school supplies budget, no real purchases or financial commitments.
Age adaptation: 6–9 concrete examples, 10–12 cooperative practice, 13–17 increasingly independent reasoning with trusted adult support, 18+ optional supervised helper pathway separate from clinical credentials. UNICEF 15–19 guide not assumed suitable for all ages. No required personal disclosure or psychological tests; no therapeutic exercises, clinical diagnosis, spiritual scoring, ranking, surveillance or compulsory service. Assess fictional task comprehension, not mental state. Accessibility, consent, source rights and qualified educator/clinician review before release. Free plain-language, low-bandwidth/offline and print delivery; no app deployment or promise of free professional care. No new branch or main merge.
