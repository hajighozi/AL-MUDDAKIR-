# R150.52C — Psychological evidence and intervention atlas
Status PLANNED; public source discovery 2026-11-28, not clinical implementation. Owner B10, cross-links B13, B11, B14/B17, B20. Additive to R150.52A/B.
## Verified public publication leads (search abstracts/official summaries; no full manual or RCT audit)
- WHO mhGAP depression structured treatment: https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/treatment-care/mental-health-gap-action-programme/evidence-centre/depression/brief-structured-psychological-treatment — adult moderate/severe depression; several structured therapies recommended. Do not generalize across age or diagnoses.
- WHO mhGAP anxiety: https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/treatment-care/mental-health-gap-action-programme/evidence-centre/anxiety/brief-structured-psychological-interventions — CBT-based approaches for adult generalized anxiety/panic.
- NICE NG134: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng134/chapter/Recommendations — child/adolescent depression; appropriately trained health professionals, age and needs matter. UK guideline not Indonesian authorization.
- WHO implementation guidance: https://www.who.int/news/item/11-03-2024-who-launches-new-manual-to-support-delivery-of-psychological-interventions — trained supervised non-specialists in organized care, not unsupervised volunteers.
- WHO/UNICEF HAT: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240025554 — prevention, supportive environments, caregiver support.
- WHO/UNICEF EASE: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240082755 — structured intervention for ages 10–15 in adversity; professional program evaluation only, no reproduction or self-delivery.
- WHO EQUIP https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240105935 — adult helper training and supervision.
- WHO PFA https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789241548205 — humane practical initial support, separate from psychotherapy.
- WHO Doing What Matters https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927 — public stress literacy source, not replacement for care.
- WHO community services https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240025707 — rights, choice, person-centred care.

## Public vs supervised scope
Public: mental health literacy, respectful language, healthy everyday habits, seeking support, safe inclusive education, navigation of existing services. Youth never responsible for crisis handling or treating peers. Adults supervised: role boundaries, PFA, referral, nonclinical practical support. Clinical: qualified professionals or properly trained supervised staff in authorized programs; no automatic credential from reading manual. Spiritual support optional; no equation of disease with sin or low faith, no replacement of clinical care. Urgent danger: immediate trusted adult and professional emergency care.
