## Ghazali primary-text lead
Ihya, Kitab Adab al-Ulfah wa al-Ukhuwwah wa al-Suhbah wa al-Mu'asharah, book five of quarter al-Adat; chapter 2 rights of companionship. Digital text indexed juz 5 p168 at https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/2472/169 (search snippet available; direct open cache miss; edition not verified). Opening p140 indexed https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/2472/141?lang=ar . Cross-check public-domain text in Arabic Wikisource, chapter 2, including headings rights 3,5,8. Eight rights: material generosity, practical assistance, restraint in speech, constructive speech, forgiveness, prayer, loyalty/sincerity, easing burdens. Distinguish ethical exhortation and legal duty; do not adopt historical anecdotal extremes, financial dependency, unquestioning allegiance, forced forgiveness, secrecy about harm or social isolation. ## Transferable design hypotheses
Shared purpose, hospitality, voluntary recurring gatherings, meaningful learning, reciprocal practical help, intergenerational relationships, participatory decision making, accessible dispute resolution, outside relationships and independent safeguarding. A community may preserve identity without enforced sameness. Family-like care does not replace families or trained professional services. Adult monastic traditions not copied into youth settings.
## Pilot proposed
One open community activity per week, family participation optional, supervised age-appropriate learning, art or shared meal and safe service. No private confessions, faith ratings, compulsory contributions, hazardous practices or pressure to remain. Track observable collaboration, ability to ask for help and decline, accessibility, learning quality and feedback. Rights and resources from source, not a claim of validated best model.
