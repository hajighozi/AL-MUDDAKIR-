# R150.30B — International community principles for Ikhwan
2026-11-27. Comparative desk research, draft; not a systematic review, universal ranking, or validation of Throwbadly. User asks for principles of the best international family-like communities. Criteria: relationships, human development, sustainability, governance, safeguarding. Current implementation scope remains Sumatera.
## Benchmarks and evidence
- Search Institute developmental relationships: care, growth, support, shared power, expanded possibilities. Organizational framework. https://searchinstitute.org/resources-hub/developmental-relationships-framework
- WOSM Scout Method: teams, adult support, community participation, learning through activity; adapt civilian education only. https://learn.scout.org/resource/scout-method and https://sdgs.scout.org/about/nso
- US 4-H: belonging, mastery, independence, generosity. https://www.nifa.usda.gov/4-h/national-headquarters/learning and https://4h.extension.wisc.edu/resources/volunteer-resources/4-h-foundations/essential-elements-of-positive-youth-development/
- Japanese Tokkatsu: class activities, student council, clubs, school events; documented practice, not causal proof. https://tokkatsu.jp/index.html
- Cohousing (origin Denmark): shared facilities and private households; 2020 scoping review, most of 22 studies examining psychosocial determinants reported positive associations; limited causal evidence. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33042597/
- L'Arche: dignity, reciprocity, inclusion of persons with intellectual disabilities. https://www.larche.org/about-larche/identity-and-mission/ . Critical counterexample: independent commission published January 2023 on founder abuse and control; institutional response acknowledged failure. https://commissiondetude-jeanvanier.org/commissiondetudeindependante2023-empriseetabus/index.php/en/home-english/ and https://www.larche.org.uk/jean-vanier-report . No idealization of founder or entire institution; external safeguarding mandatory.
- UNICEF UK child rights approach: best interests, development, non-discrimination, participation, dignity, interdependence of rights, transparency/accountability. https://www.unicef.org.uk/child-friendly-cities/crba/
- Ostrom principles for commons: participatory rulemaking, accountable governance, accessible dispute resolution, local autonomy and nested organization. Adaptation hypothesis, not evidence for youth groups or permission to police peers. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167268112002697
## Research basis
McMillan & Chavis 1986: membership, influence, needs fulfillment and shared emotional connection; conceptual model. https://www.drdavidmcmillan.com/sense-of-community/article-1
Self-determination theory: autonomy, competence, relatedness. https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/topics/application-basic-psychological-needs/
CDC school connectedness: protective association and supportive school strategies. https://www.cdc.gov/youth-behavior/school-connectedness/
Raposa et al. 2019, 70 studies, 25,286 youth, significant overall mentoring effects; no direct validation of murabbi/tarekat. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30661211/
Burton et al. 2022, six cross-age mentoring studies, g=.45; adult oversight associated with larger effects. Small evidence base, no comparison of universal community models. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34965319/
## Proposed asas Ikhwan (not final user decisions)
1 Shared purpose (ridha Allah and benefit, without compulsory affiliation).
2 Rahmah and dignity independent of achievement.
3 Belonging without exclusion or forced intimacy.
4 Reciprocal assistance and access to trusted adults.
5 Learning and development of talents.
6 Autonomy, voice, freedom to disagree and decline.
7 Everyday shared experiences: meals, reading, art, play, safe service.
8 Fair musyawarah, transparent rules and accountable adult governance.
9 Protection, privacy, independent reporting and no retaliation.
10 Openness to families and wider society; intergroup cooperation, no isolation.
11 Local adaptation, continuity and shared resources without financial coercion.
12 Reflection, evidence, feedback and correction of mistakes.
## Pilot concept
Optional small group (proposed 6–8 peers, not validated optimum), age-appropriate activities, trained adult supervision, family involvement, independent safeguarding contact, inclusive access. One short weekly gathering (proposed cadence), shared reading/dialogue or art and small safe community project. Private reflections never required. Assess aggregate belonging, safety, voice, help-seeking, collaboration and concrete learning; no faith, loyalty, disclosure or addiction-recovery scores. No data of children in public repository. Use baseline and follow-up with consent and review; no causal efficacy claims without appropriate design.
