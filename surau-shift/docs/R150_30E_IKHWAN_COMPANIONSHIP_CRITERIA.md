Five criteria in text: عقل، حسن الخلق، غير فاسق، غير مبتدع، غير حريص على الدنيا. Ghazali connects suitability of companionship to intended benefits, distinguishes understanding from conduct, describes different kinds of friends and purposes, and discusses context-dependent responses to wrongdoing. These are historical religious ethical categories, not psychological diagnoses or admission scores. No reproduction of modern translations. Do not treat disagreement, disability, poverty, mental health difficulties or religious identity as evidence of moral inferiority; no shunning, coercion, peer discipline or loyalty testing. Distinguish maintaining goodwill from remaining in harmful relationships.

## Operational hypotheses
A healthy friendship offers trust, reciprocal learning, respect for boundaries, willingness to repair mistakes, and mutual benefit. Choice remains voluntary; allow diverse friendships and time alone. No single friend should carry every need. Adults provide safeguarding and competent advice; friends can support help-seeking, not replace family, professionals or independent channels. Optional shared learning, recreation, hospitality and safe service can create conditions for friendship without assigning exclusive partners or ranking people. Assess activity quality and voluntary aggregate feedback on safety, voice, cooperation and access to help, never faith, loyalty or intimate disclosures.
