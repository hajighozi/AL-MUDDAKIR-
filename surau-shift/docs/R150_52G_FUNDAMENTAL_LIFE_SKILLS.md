LU-HS01–08 proposed, not new KUs or clinical qualifications. Reuse prior LU-LS02 where relevant; do not count duplicate content as unique. Age 6–9 concrete choices, 10–12 simple fictional situations, 13–17 compare options and practice help-seeking in routine scenarios, adults wider service-navigation education. No personal disclosures required. No youth crisis response, diagnosis, therapy, symptom screening, psychological scoring, spiritual scoring, counseling chatbot or confidentiality promises in safety situations. Serious/urgent danger -> trusted adult and emergency professional care. Adult and professional responsibilities remain separate. Qualified educators, clinician/safeguarding reviewers and local service verification required before release.
## Delivery
Free low-bandwidth reading, printable cards and local language after review. No sign-in or private patient data. P3LP is supportive first aid, not a replacement for clinical care. No guarantee free therapy or recovery. No app changes, deployment, main merge, or claim of validated effectiveness.
