## Proposed ten foundations
Hadd: study of practical religious rulings and ethical judgments concerning responsible invitation to Allah, with contextual understanding and qualified interpretation.
Mawdu: acts of invitation and communication by humans, not persons' inner faith.
Thamara: informed, truthful, compassionate and accountable practice.
Nisba: applied intersection of fiqh/usul, ethics, tafsir, hadith, pedagogy and social sciences; not equivalent to all communication science.
Fadl: virtue of lawful beneficial counsel, not prestige or certification of piety.
Wadi: no single founder established; classical foundations and later systematic literature.
Ism: Fiqh al-Da'wah; distinguish Ilm al-Da'wah, Usul al-Da'wah, methods, hisbah and professional service.
Istimdad: Quran, Sunnah, qualified fiqh/usul, relevant empirical context (not independent source of religious rulings).
Hukm: learning relevant personal duties according to need; specialized scholarship and teaching have different requirements. No blanket fard ayn for full capstone; consult qualified scholars.
Masail: scope/authority, intention, accuracy, audience rights, context, priority, consequences, disagreement, family, professional example, accountability.
## A-H proposal
A acts of invitation; B legal-ethical consideration; C applicable rulings conditional on circumstances and competence; D questions of accurate communication, consent, priority, consequences; E source interpretation, harm avoidance, truthful speech; F sources above with partial verification; G separate preaching from professional licensing, clinical care and coercive enforcement; H candidate applied branch of Ilm al-Dawah, relation to fiqh/usul across domains, independent science not established. Do not create new ID in 398 corpus or infer membership before original audit. Capstone is curricular role, not taxonomy.
## Khidmah readiness
Observable contribution with agreed need, suitable competence, safety, outcome evidence, independent feedback, reflection/correction. No wealth, follower count, conversion targets, spiritual rating or compulsory unpaid work. A young learner's safe service is not professional certification. All four DNA continue throughout life; ordinary advice within knowledge and adab remains possible before capstone. Family first, voluntary participation, respect freedom of belief, no manipulation or coercion.
