# R-150 addendum — FAHM depth audit
Status C/draft, requested deepening; audit scope only, not new curriculum approval. R-133 defines L0–L4 learning competencies, but FAHM as a DNA is broader than L2. Distinguish comprehension of text, concepts, evidence, reasoning, context, implications and limits. Source passages checked at verse level: https://quran.com/17/36 (responsibility for knowledge), https://quran.com/47/24 (tadabbur); https://quran.com/62/2 and https://quran.com/2/129 (tilawah, teaching and tazkiyah in differing textual orders). Pedagogical interpretation is a proposal, not a tafsir consensus or empirically demonstrated sequence.
Audit questions: (1) what is meant by a term (tasawwur, definition, example/nonexample); (2) what is asserted (tasdiq, claim and warrant); (3) what source and method fit the jihat; (4) what alternatives, ambiguity and limits exist; (5) how can a learner explain in their own words and apply to a new case; (6) how can a learner revise without shame. Distinguish tadabbur from authoritative tafsir and empirical observation from religious claims. Keep M0–M7, A–H and source→claim→KU→LU separate. Candidate source research: classical mantiq, usul, adab al-bahth; audit exact edition and passage, do not attribute unverified terms. X–Y–Z only when meaningful. No claim of completion of original corpus. Suggested demonstration: explain a plant observation, separate observation from inference, then correct after new evidence. Rubrics assess explanation, evidence, transfer, correction; never spiritual rank. Coordinate with Surau Shift, no changes to registry or engine before source gate.
