## Typed distinctions and open conflicts
RSIC-01 (S1 p323 vs S2 M7-BD): model 1–3–2 = Qauliyah (revelation/value orientation), Nafsiyah (akal/qalb processor), Kauniyah (empirical inquiry). A historical conceptual model, not a validated causal sequence, not domain taxonomy. Separate source, method, faculty and object; empirical evidence does not by itself establish metaphysical claims. Original theory and citations unresolved.
RSIC-02 (S1 p324): RSI-X = visible manifestation X, structure Y, function Z, origin R, impact W. Five question prompts, not automatically five disciplines or identical to Matan/Syarah X–Y–Z. R metaphysical/historical/causal questions need distinct evidence. Compare Jihat, no forced equivalence; original X theory document needed.
RSIC-03 (S1 pp404–405 vs S4): four curriculum strands Ruh/Akal/Khidmah/Peradaban are educational content/organization; four DNA IQRA/FAHM/TAZKIYAH/KHIDMAH are interwoven processes. Many-to-many link, not stages: Ruh uses all DNA; Akal emphasizes IQRA/FAHM with adab/practice; Khidmah uses all DNA; Peradaban integrates learning and social application. Mapping is AI proposal, not original curriculum detail.
RSIC-04 (S1 pp399–406,334): five historical journeys: Anak (Aku Dicintai Allah), Remaja (Aku Berarti), Pemuda (Aku Melayani), Kader (Aku Bertanggung Jawab), Murabbi (Aku Menumbuhkan Manusia). Earlier p399 uses Aku Membahagiakan for adults. p334 age bands and role labels are draft, no automatic eligibility, spiritual hierarchy or obligatory profession. p402 phrase that murabbi no longer learns conflicts with lifelong learning direction. Retain variant; proposed correction ongoing learning and competence-based adult roles. No minor responsible for peers' safety, discipline, economic production or care duties.
RSIC-05 (S1 pp509–511): seven aspirational profiles God-Conscious Individual, Prophetic Follower, Person of Adab, Steward of Trust, Community Servant, Mentor & Builder, Person of Excellence. p336 extraction only four Arabic labels visible (Abdullah, Sahibul Adab, Shahibul Amanah, Khadimul Ummah), don't invent other labels or assert exact equivalence. Distinguish goals from observed learning outcomes; no scoring belief, inner feelings, divine favor, compulsory disclosures or clinical claims. Murabbi not universal graduation requirement.
RSIC-06 (S1 p398 vs S2 M7-B and S3 p2): RSI faculties Qauliyah/Kauniyah/Nafsiyah/Sistem/Sosial; M7 working domains Qauliyah/Kauniyah/Nafsiyah/Ijtima'iyah/Aqliyah; Master Plan Qauliyah Qur'an, Nafsiyah Nabi via hadis/sirah, Kauniyah alam, Ijtima' masyarakat/sejarah, Nizhamiyah/Aqliyah patterns. Six apps include Lughah as cross-field tool. Link as distinct entities; no automatic synonym. Retain ONT01–04 and DEL06; unresolved naming and boundaries. M7 Aqliyah as object differs from aql/nazhar as Madarik; no claim universal classical five-domain classification.
RSIC-07 (S1 p403 vs S3 p2): Sarang Lebah historical method and current program branch of Surau Shift; preserve both roles and distinguish method from institution. Current Sumatera scope prevails over historical broader geography.
RSIC-08 (S2 M3 12–14, S3 pp4–5): preserve 16 Learning Unit components and five assessment aspects. Source→KU→LU does not establish causal effect. RSI strands/journeys/profiles may become tagged metadata for LU, with explicit source, relation type and review status. 12 cycles are pilot proposal, not all-age RSI curriculum. Do not replace them or assign mandatory progression.
