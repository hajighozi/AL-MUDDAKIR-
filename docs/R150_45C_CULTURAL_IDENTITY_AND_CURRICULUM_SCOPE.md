The shared curriculum deepens IQRA/FAHM, TAZKIYAH, KHIDMAH; Kitab IQRA is its worldview. Names follow the culture and place of local educators and contributors. Distinguish philosophical applicability across cultures from actual Throwbadly work scope (Sumatera). No global operational expansion authorized by this clarification.
